maim

Mouse aim.

Click squares as they travel from left to right. Each hit removes one target and adds one point. Start, pause or reset whenever you like.

Based on a small Python game made while enjoying first-person shooters. This is mouse practice, not a calibrated reaction-time test or a promise of better in-game performance. Touch works too; aiming requires a pointing device.

Play stays on this device. No scores are saved or sent, and there are no accounts or analytics. GitHub Pages processes ordinary hosting requests.
